## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for higher education institutions, deans, department heads, and faculty members who demand immediate clarity, rigorous precision, and friction-free daily operation. The interface must balance academic authority with high-velocity product ergonomics found in developer-grade SaaS tools like Linear, Notion, and Stripe Dashboard.

The visual style is **Corporate Modern Minimalist**:
- High-density data presentation without visual clutter or unnecessary decorative elements.
- Crisp horizontal and vertical alignment lines that reflect academic rigor and operational order.
- Fast, tactile interactions where state shifts (such as marking an entire lecture cohort present) happen with micro-feedback rather than heavy modal flows.
- Utilitarian neutrality: Chrome recedes to the background, letting status indicators, attendance trends, and analytical charts take immediate visual precedence.

## Colors

The palette establishes institutional clarity through a high-contrast structure grounded in tech-forward slate neutrals, vivid status states, and a dependable electric cobalt primary.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Applied deliberately to high-priority interactive touchpoints: active batch attendance triggers, primary submission buttons, selected tab indicators, and progress bars.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Dark slate navy reserved for high-contrast headers, structural navigation sidebars, and critical system alerts.
- **Neutrals**:
  - `Canvas / Background`: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
  - `Surface Containers / Table Rows`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `Subtle Row Hovers & Inactive Tabs`: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
  - `Dividers & Ghost Borders`: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
  - `Muted Text & Column Captions`: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
  - `Primary Text`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)

### Attendance Status Semantics
Color is systematically paired with persistent text labels to maintain strict WCAG AAA accessibility:
- **Present (`#16A34A`)**: Background tint `#F0FDF4`, border `#BBF7D0`, text `#15803D`.
- **Absent (`#DC2626`)**: Background tint `#FEF2F2`, border `#FECACA`, text `#B91C1C`.
- **Late (`#F59E0B`)**: Background tint `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#B45309`.
- **Excused (`#6366F1`)**: Background tint `#EEF2FF`, border `#C7D2FE`, text `#4338CA`.

## Typography

Typography relies on `Inter` across all functional roles to provide a systematic, clean, and legible hierarchy.

### Rules of Engagement
- **Numerical Alignment**: Use tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across student roll numbers, attendance percentages, dates, and timestamp columns to keep data grids mathematically aligned.
- **Section Headers & Captions**: Category tags and table column heads utilize `label-sm` with uppercase transformation and a slight letter-spacing boost (`0.04em`) to establish crisp section delineation.
- **Readability Rules**: Body text line height adheres tightly to a 1.4-1.5x ratio to optimize dense student lists and session schedules without causing visual fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a hybrid 12-column responsive grid paired with persistent navigation regions.

### Structure & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and above)**: Collapsible 260px left sidebar alongside a 12-column fluid grid, 2rem canvas margins, and 1.5rem gutters. Maximum container width capped at 1600px for ultra-wide monitors to preserve ocular sweep efficiency.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with 1.25rem gutters. Sidebar collapses into an icon rail (64px) or slide-over drawer.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout with 1rem canvas margins and 0.75rem gutters. High-density data tables collapse into stacked cards with segmented quick-toggle controls.

### Density Philosophy
Internal component padding enforces rhythm:
- Compact data rows rely on `space-sm` (8px) vertical padding.
- Metric overview cards and session details utilize `space-lg` (24px) perimeter padding to frame metrics clearly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via structural surface layers, subtle tonal transitions, and razor-sharp borders rather than heavy blur shadows, echoing the Stripe Dashboard and Linear paradigm.

### Layer Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (`#F8FAFC`)**: Base foundational viewport background.
2. **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary work surfaces—tables, content cards, sidebars, and tab containers. Framed by a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`).
3. **Elevated Containers / Dropdowns**: Hover cards, select menus, and filter popovers receive a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-spread micro-shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
4. **Modals & Command Palettes**: Centered elevated sheets sitting above a 40% opacity slate scrim (`#0F172A` with 40% alpha), framed with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

### Border Integrity
Every card, tab group, and data container must have a distinct `#E2E8F0` hairline separation to ensure strong edge detection under varied ambient lighting in classrooms and lecture halls.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp enterprise efficiency with modern software warmth through rounded geometric curves.

### Shape Tokens & Roles
- **Base Surfaces & Cards (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px internal standard)**: Outer bounds of analytical panels, schedule cards, and table wrappers.
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Data Cells (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem / 8px)**: Interactive operational inputs, select pickers, and individual cell controls.
- **Quick Action Pills & Status Badges (`rounded-full` / 9999px)**: State badges (Present, Absent, Late, Excused) and bulk filter pills use full pill caps to differentiate states from structured cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Quick Action Controls
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#2563EB` fill, white text, 8px radius, height 36px (desktop) / 44px (touch target mobile). Subtle hover shift to `#1D4ED8`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Quick Action Pills**: Segmented button groups for fast attendance toggling. When marking a student:
  - Default: Slate outline `#E2E8F0` with neutral text.
  - Active: Instantly illuminates in the corresponding status background, text, and border tokens with a snappy 100ms ease-out transition.

### Status Badges
- Compact inline pills with 6px vertical and 10px horizontal padding.
- Consists of a 6px circular dot indicator followed by semibold text (`Present`, `Absent`, `Late`, `Excused`).
- High-contrast text matches the respective semantic foreground color tokens to ensure compliance against both desktop screens and low-tier classroom projectors.

### Data Tables & Roster Lists
- **Header**: `#F8FAFC` background, 36px height, uppercase `label-sm` typography with sort directional arrows.
- **Rows**: `#FFFFFF` background with 1px border-bottom (`#F1F5F9`). Hover triggers background switch to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Row Anatomy**: Leftmost cell holds a multi-select checkbox followed by student avatar, name, and matriculation ID. Right-aligned columns host the 4-way attendance quick-toggle pill group and historical percentage bar.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl` corners, framed by 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- Metric cards display aggregate attendance metrics, sparklines, and percentage change labels with dedicated icons in the top-right corner.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- Clean inputs with 8px radius, height 38px, `#FFFFFF` fill, and `#CBD5E1` border.
- Active focus state: `#2563EB` hairline border accompanied by a 3px outer ring tinted to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`. No default browser outlines.

### Tab Bars & Segmented Views
- Horizontal track encased in `#F1F5F9` container with 6px padding.
- Selected tab features `#FFFFFF` background, subtle 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), and `#0F172A` medium-weight text, simulating a physical elevated tab switch.
